# Melbourne Attractions Overview

Federation Square is Melbourne's civic square, rated 4.1, hosting events, galleries, and public screens. It is the lowest-rated sight in the Melbourne set but is still recommended as a central meeting point, since it gets very busy during scheduled events.

The Melbourne Street Art Laneway Tour is a guided walk through Hosier Lane and nearby graffiti-covered laneways, rated 4.6. Reviewers particularly value guides who share background stories about individual artists rather than just walking past the art.

Chin Chin Melbourne is a loud, popular modern Thai/Asian restaurant on Flinders Lane, rated 4.4. It is well known for weekend crowds, and reviewers recommend booking ahead rather than walking in.

Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria is a sprawling garden on the Yarra River, rated 4.7 and free to enter, making it the highest-rated Melbourne sight and one of the highest-rated attractions overall in the app's data.

Queen Victoria Market is a historic open-air market rated 4.3 with food stalls, produce, and deli halls. It is categorised as a restaurant in this app because most visits centre on eating rather than sightseeing, and reviewers recommend arriving before 9am for parking.

The Great Ocean Road Day Trip is a full-day coastal drive from Melbourne past the Twelve Apostles, rated 4.8. It is the highest-rated activity in the entire dataset; reviewers describe it as a long day but say the views are unforgettable.
