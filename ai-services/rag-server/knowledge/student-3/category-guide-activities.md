# Category Guide: Activities

"Activity" covers attractions built around doing something over a period of time, rather than viewing a fixed landmark or eating: guided walks, day trips, and venues you explore rather than simply visit. The Bondi to Coogee Coastal Walk, Taronga Zoo Sydney, the Melbourne Street Art Laneway Tour, and the Great Ocean Road Day Trip are all activities in the current dataset.

Activities in this dataset have the highest average rating of the three categories: 4.8, 4.5, 4.6, and 4.8 respectively, all at or above 4.5. This reflects that reviewers tend to rate a well-executed multi-hour experience highly when it delivers on its premise, though it also means a high activity rating does not by itself distinguish a free walk (Bondi to Coogee) from a paid, full-day tour (Great Ocean Road Day Trip).

Time commitment varies widely within this category: the Bondi to Coogee walk can be done in a few hours, while the Great Ocean Road Day Trip and guided laneway tours are scheduled, multi-hour or full-day commitments that are harder to fit into a short visit.
