# Accessibility Notes

Large, purpose-built sights such as the Sydney Opera House and Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria are generally the most accessible category in this dataset, with paved paths and step-free entry suited to wheelchair users and strollers.

Walking-based activities vary widely in accessibility: the Bondi to Coogee Coastal Walk includes uneven, sloped clifftop sections that are not wheelchair-friendly end-to-end, while the flatter city sections of the Melbourne Street Art Laneway Tour are easier to navigate but still involve cobblestones in places like Hosier Lane.

Markets such as The Rocks Markets and Queen Victoria Market can be difficult to navigate with a wheelchair or large stroller during peak hours, since stalls narrow the walkways and crowd density increases along with popularity.

Sit-down restaurants (Chat Thai Sydney CBD, Mr. Wong, Chin Chin Melbourne) are the most consistently accessible category for mobility needs, since they are indoor venues with standard table seating, though peak-time queues can still be a barrier for visitors who cannot stand and wait.
