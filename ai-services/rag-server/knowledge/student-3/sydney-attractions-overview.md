# Sydney Attractions Overview

The Sydney Opera House is the app's highest-rated sight in Sydney at 4.7 stars. It is a sail-shaped performing arts venue on Sydney Harbour, best known for its architecture and harbour views. Visitors consistently mention it is very crowded, especially around sunset, but rate the experience as worth the crowds.

The Bondi to Coogee Coastal Walk is a 6km clifftop walking track past Sydney's most famous beaches, rated 4.8. It is the highest-rated activity in the Sydney set and is frequently recommended as a free, low-cost way to spend a morning; reviewers suggest starting early to avoid summer heat.

Chat Thai Sydney CBD is a busy, no-frills Thai restaurant rated 4.3, popular with locals and students for being cheap and fast without sacrificing quality.

Taronga Zoo Sydney is a harbourside zoo rated 4.5 with ferry access and views back to the Sydney skyline from across the harbour. Reviewers note it is a good family activity, though ticket prices are on the higher side for a full-day visit.

The Rocks Markets is a historic cobblestone precinct in Sydney rated 4.2, hosting weekend craft and food stalls. It is a sight rather than a restaurant in this app's categorisation, since the main appeal is the historic precinct itself rather than any single vendor.

Mr. Wong is an upscale Cantonese restaurant rated 4.6 in a converted Sydney CBD warehouse, known among reviewers for its duck dishes.
