# Category Guide: Sights

In the Local Experience & Attraction Recommender, "sight" covers attractions whose main appeal is a place to see or a landmark to visit, rather than a meal or a scheduled activity. The Sydney Opera House, The Rocks Markets, Federation Square, and Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria are all classified as sights.

A venue built around eating, like Queen Victoria Market, is classified as a restaurant instead even though it is technically outdoors and market-like, because most visits centre on food. This means "sight" in this app is about the primary reason someone visits, not the physical format of the venue.

Sights in the current dataset range from free (Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria, at no entry cost) to ticketed (Sydney Opera House, for performances and tours). Category alone does not tell you the price or the crowd level — check the individual attraction's description and reviews for that detail.
