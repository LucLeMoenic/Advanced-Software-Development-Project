# Category Guide: Restaurants

"Restaurant" in this app covers any attraction where the main reason to visit is to eat, including sit-down restaurants like Chat Thai Sydney CBD, Mr. Wong, and Chin Chin Melbourne, and food-focused markets like Queen Victoria Market.

Restaurant ratings in the seeded dataset range from 4.3 (Chat Thai Sydney CBD, Queen Victoria Market) to 4.6 (Mr. Wong), all comfortably above the halfway point of the app's 1-5 rating scale, so a rating alone does not distinguish a casual, cheap option from a fine-dining one — the description and price positioning matter more.

Busy, popular restaurants such as Chin Chin Melbourne and Mr. Wong are noted in reviews as needing advance booking, while casual options such as Chat Thai Sydney CBD are described as fast and no-frills with no booking expected.
