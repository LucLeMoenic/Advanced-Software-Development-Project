# Opening Hours and Planning

Markets and weekend-oriented attractions in this dataset, such as The Rocks Markets and Queen Victoria Market, run on a limited weekly schedule rather than every day, so they are best planned around a weekend visit rather than a weekday trip.

Sights with regular daily opening hours, such as Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria and Federation Square, are suited to being added to a same-day plan alongside a nearby restaurant, since they do not require advance date-specific booking the way a guided tour does.

Scheduled activities such as the Melbourne Street Art Laneway Tour and the Great Ocean Road Day Trip run at fixed departure times and should be booked ahead of the visit date, since reviewers note that walk-up availability is unreliable, especially on weekends.

Popular restaurants that reviewers describe as needing a booking, including Chin Chin Melbourne and Mr. Wong, should be planned around a reservation window rather than an arbitrary meal time; casual, no-booking options like Chat Thai Sydney CBD are better suited to spontaneous, unplanned visits.
