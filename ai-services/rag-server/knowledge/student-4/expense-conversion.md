# Expense entry and conversion snapshots
Source: `student-4/backend/Api/BudgetEndpoints.cs`, `student-4/backend/Services/FrankfurterExchangeRateProvider.cs`, `student-4/backend/Program.cs`; Frankfurter, "Frankfurter | Free exchange rates API", https://www.frankfurter.dev/ | Accessed: 2026-10-08 | Reviewed: 2026-10-08 | Source-verified conversion behavior and original paraphrase of provider documentation; no executable exchange-rate quotes.

Record an expense against its budget with a description, positive amount, original currency, and date inside the budget period. To convert and save an expense, the backend uses decimal reference rates from Frankfurter's ECB provider, rounds once to minor units with midpoint values away from zero, and saves the converted amount with the applied rate and rate date as a historical snapshot. Reading saved expenses does not refresh their conversions. Editing only a description, notes or purchase date keeps the saved snapshot; changing the amount, original currency or budget base currency requires a new conversion. The rate date is the provider's observation date, not necessarily the purchase date.

Frankfurter supplies daily reference exchange rates, not live market rates or a promise of card-payment rates. Check the returned rate date; a latest reference rate is not a real-time quote. Card providers and currency exchanges may apply different rates or fees. Existing saved expenses retain their historical rate snapshots, including older fixed demonstration rates, until a conversion-changing edit. This guidance cannot promise an exchange rate for tomorrow or infer what your bank will charge.
