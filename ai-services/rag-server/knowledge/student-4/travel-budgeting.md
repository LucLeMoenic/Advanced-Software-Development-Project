# Practical travel spending and category choices
Source: ASIC Moneysmart, "Track your spending", https://moneysmart.gov.au/budgeting/track-your-spending (updated 2026-07-30), and "How to do a budget", https://moneysmart.gov.au/budgeting/how-to-do-a-budget (updated 2026-08-31); Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC), "Price displays", https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/pricing/price-displays, and "Travel delays and cancellations", https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/specific-products-and-activities/travel-delays-and-cancellations; tracker categories: `student-4/backend/Api/Validation.cs` | Accessed: 2026-10-08 | Reviewed: 2026-10-08 | Original paraphrases adapted to travel and the supported tracker categories; comparison checklists are general suggestions, not personal diagnoses, quotes or guarantees.

To reduce spending on a trip, review your recorded purchases, including small cash payments. Separate essential spending from optional spending before choosing a cut. Start with one change, such as planning meals instead of buying food you will waste, or delaying an optional purchase. Set a limit for discretionary spending and check it regularly; protect essentials rather than assuming every expense can be removed.

If your accommodation (accomodation) spending looks high, review the full stay cost rather than only the advertised nightly price. Check the number of nights, booking fees, taxes and optional extras against your receipts. Compare accommodation options for the same dates and guests using their complete prices. Before switching, check cancellation terms and any loss of payments already made. This guidance cannot see your bookings or establish why your own accommodation total is high.

To decide what category you should add, look at the purchases you expect on your trip. Use accommodation for a room, food for meals, transport for fares, activities for entry tickets, shopping for gifts, and other for costs that do not fit those categories. Add a positive planned limit and date period for each selected category. Keep separate kinds of spending visible instead of putting every purchase under other. Choose the category that matches your next expected purchase; not every category applies to every journey.

In the tracker, you can update category budgets for accommodation, food, transport, activities, shopping and other. To update categories, review the planned limits and date periods against your travel plans and recorded expenses. Custom categories are not supported: the category labels are fixed, so add a budget for an existing category, not a new custom category name. Raising a planned limit is not a spending reduction; review optional purchases as well. This guidance cannot inspect or update your saved budgets.
