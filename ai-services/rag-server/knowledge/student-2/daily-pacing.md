# Daily pacing and avoiding rushed itineraries
Project-authored general planning guidance, October 2026; not measured travel times or a personalised capacity assessment.

To avoid a rushed or overloaded itinerary, choose a small number of priority activities for each day and leave optional stops that can be dropped. Group nearby attractions in the same neighbourhood to reduce backtracking. Include time for travel between stops, meals, rest breaks, queues and getting lost; do not fill every hour with an activity. Place a must-do activity or a fixed reservation first, then arrange flexible stops around it. A relaxed day can use one main activity with optional nearby stops, rather than treating a fixed number of attractions as a target. Keep arrival and departure days lighter. If a day feels too busy, remove a lower-priority stop or move it to a quieter day instead of shortening every visit. Exact feasibility requires actual distances, opening hours and each traveller's pace; the saved stop list alone cannot prove that a day is feasible.
