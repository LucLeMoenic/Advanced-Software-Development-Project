# Packing and practical trip preparation
Project-authored general planning guidance, October 2026; not entry, airline baggage or medical requirements.

Build a packing checklist around trip length, planned activities, expected conditions and access to laundry. Choose reusable layers and comfortable footwear suited to the planned walking; include sun or rain protection when appropriate to the forecast. Check the transport operator's current baggage allowance and restricted-item rules before packing. Keep essential travel documents, booking details, contact information and a charger accessible, with secure offline copies where useful. Check the destination's plug type and your device's voltage compatibility before choosing an adapter. Carry personally needed medication according to professional advice and verify destination and carrier rules rather than assuming it is permitted. Check passport, visa and entry requirements through official sources for the traveller's nationality and route. This checklist cannot establish eligibility to enter a country, prescribe medication, or provide current baggage limits or destination-specific electrical specifications.
