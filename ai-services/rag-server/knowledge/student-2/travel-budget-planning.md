# Planning travel expenses and a contingency budget
Project-authored general planning guidance, October 2026; not price data or financial advice.

To plan travel expenses, split the available money into accommodation, transport, food, paid activities and a contingency for unexpected costs. Separate fixed costs already committed from flexible spending, and set aside the contingency before allocating money to optional activities. Keep estimates distinct from confirmed prices and check what is included in each booking, such as baggage, meals, transfers and cancellation charges. To reduce costs, prioritise a few paid experiences, group nearby stops to reduce transport costs and mix paid activities with options verified as free. Track actual spending separately and adjust discretionary purchases when estimates change. Choose a contingency appropriate to the traveller's plans and risk tolerance; this guidance prescribes no fixed amount or percentage. These are planning suggestions, not expense-tracking features of the app or live price quotes.
