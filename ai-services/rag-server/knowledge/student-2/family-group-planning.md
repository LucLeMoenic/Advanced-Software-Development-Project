# Family and group itinerary planning
Project-authored general planning guidance, October 2026; not venue suitability, supervision or health advice.

When planning a trip with children or a mixed-interest group, agree on a few shared priorities and ask about energy levels, food needs, mobility and preferred pace. Alternate demanding activities with easier stops, include meal and rest breaks, and leave flexibility for naps or an early finish. Keep travel between activities manageable and avoid making every day depend on several timed bookings. Let different interests have space through optional activities rather than requiring everyone to do every stop. Agree on a meeting place and a way to contact one another if the group splits, with appropriate supervision for children. Confirm age restrictions, facilities, dietary options and ticket conditions directly with venues; do not assume an activity is child-friendly from its name. For mixed budgets, agree on spending expectations before booking shared activities. No fixed daily stop count or schedule works for every family or group.
