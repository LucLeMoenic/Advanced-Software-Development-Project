# Accessibility and mobility-aware itinerary planning
Project-authored general planning guidance, October 2026; not verified venue accessibility or a medical assessment.

For a wheelchair-accessible or mobility-aware itinerary, start with the traveller's own access needs and preferred pace rather than assuming that one accessibility label suits everyone. Verify step-free entrances, lifts, accessible toilets, seating, surface conditions and the complete route between transport and each venue with the operator. Check assistance arrangements and whether advance notice is required. Plan rest breaks and manageable distances, avoid unnecessary backtracking, and retain a backup if a lift or route is unavailable. Sensory needs may call for quieter visiting times, reduced-stimulation spaces or shorter visits; verify facilities directly. Include companions in planning only as the traveller prefers. Do not infer accessibility from an attraction's name, a map distance or the presence of public transport. The knowledge base does not certify any named venue, hotel, route or vehicle as accessible.
