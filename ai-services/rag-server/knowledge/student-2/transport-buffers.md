# Transport connections and travel buffers
Project-authored general planning guidance, October 2026; not a live route planner or timetable.

When planning transport between activities, compare walking, public transport and taxis using current local route information, considering cost, accessibility, luggage and the whole door-to-door journey. Include walking to a station, waiting, transfers and the final walk, not just time on a vehicle. Leave a travel buffer before timed tickets, connections and reservations; use a larger margin for unfamiliar routes, luggage or an important connection. There is no universal safe buffer: check the operator's current check-in, boarding and transfer requirements and allow for disruption. Avoid scheduling an essential activity immediately after a flight or long-distance arrival. Keep an alternative route and an offline copy of essential addresses and booking details. This guidance cannot confirm a departure time, fare, service frequency, fastest route or minimum connection time for a specific journey.
