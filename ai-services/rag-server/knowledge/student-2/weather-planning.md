# Weather-aware itinerary planning

For outdoor itinerary activities, check the destination weather forecast close to departure and again before the activity. Rain or thunderstorms can make an indoor alternative worth considering; check local warnings and the operator's advice before deciding. Keep outdoor plans flexible rather than treating a forecast as a guarantee. Match the forecast date and location to the planned day. A precipitation probability is not a prediction of rainfall duration or amount. Missing forecast dates do not mean dry weather, and dates beyond the forecast window cannot establish activity suitability. This is general project-authored planning guidance, reviewed October 2026, not a live forecast, local safety assessment, opening-hours source, or booking confirmation.
