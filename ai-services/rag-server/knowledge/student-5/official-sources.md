# Official Sources and Smartraveller
Last reviewed: 2026-09. Curated demonstration content for the Travel Logistics & Advisory Service: general, non-time-sensitive guidance for Australian passport holders only. It is not legal, migration or medical advice; confirm current rules with official sources before travelling.

Smartraveller is the Australian Government travel advice service run by the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT). Smartraveller publishes destination advice, safety information and entry requirement summaries for Australians travelling overseas, and you can subscribe to receive updates for a destination.

Smartraveller advice levels: each destination has one of four advice levels: Exercise normal safety precautions, Exercise a high degree of caution, Reconsider your need to travel, and Do not travel. A higher advice level can affect travel insurance cover, so check the advice level before booking and again before departure.

The destination authority is the final word on entry: visa rules, fees and permitted stays are set by the destination country and can change without notice. Confirm entry requirements on the destination official immigration website or with its embassy or consulate in Australia before paying for flights or accommodation.

This service gives general guidance only: the visa categories, weather notes and transit options shown come from curated demonstration records, not live government data. The advisory, MCP tool results and knowledge-base answers must not be treated as legal or migration advice, and every visa result carries an official-source reminder.
