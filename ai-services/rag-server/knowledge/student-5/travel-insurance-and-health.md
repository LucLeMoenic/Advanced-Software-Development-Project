# Travel Insurance and Health Preparation
Last reviewed: 2026-09. Curated demonstration content for the Travel Logistics & Advisory Service: general, non-time-sensitive guidance for Australian passport holders only. It is not legal, migration or medical advice; confirm current rules with official sources before travelling.

Travel insurance: buy travel insurance when you book, so cancellation cover starts early. Read the policy for exclusions, such as pre-existing conditions, adventure activities, alcohol-related incidents and destinations with a high Smartraveller advice level, and carry the insurer emergency contact number.

Vaccinations and health checks: see a doctor or travel health clinic several weeks before departure to discuss vaccinations and health risks for your destination. Some vaccinations need more than one dose or time to become effective, and some countries require proof of yellow fever vaccination.

Reciprocal health care: Australia has reciprocal health care agreements with a small number of countries, but these agreements cover only limited, medically necessary care and are not a substitute for travel insurance.
