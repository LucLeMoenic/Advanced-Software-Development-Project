# Passport Validity and Entry Documents
Last reviewed: 2026-09. Curated demonstration content for the Travel Logistics & Advisory Service: general, non-time-sensitive guidance for Australian passport holders only. It is not legal, migration or medical advice; confirm current rules with official sources before travelling.

Passport validity: many countries require a passport to remain valid for at least six months beyond your arrival or departure date. Airlines can refuse boarding if passport validity is too short, so check the expiry date before booking and renew early if fewer than six months would remain.

Passport condition and pages: a damaged passport, such as one with water damage or a torn page, can be refused at the border. Some countries also require one or two blank passport pages for entry stamps or a visa on arrival. Report a lost or stolen passport immediately and apply for a replacement.

Proof of onward travel: immigration officers may ask for a return or onward ticket showing you will leave within the permitted stay. Keep the onward ticket confirmation available offline, because border checks often happen before you can reach Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Supporting entry documents can include confirmed accommodation, proof of sufficient funds, a printed visa or eVisa approval, and travel insurance details. Keep paper copies and digital copies of your passport photo page and key entry documents stored separately from the originals.
