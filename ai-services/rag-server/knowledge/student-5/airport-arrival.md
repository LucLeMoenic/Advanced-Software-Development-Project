# Airport Arrival and Border Steps
Last reviewed: 2026-09. Curated demonstration content for the Travel Logistics & Advisory Service: general, non-time-sensitive guidance for Australian passport holders only. It is not legal, migration or medical advice; confirm current rules with official sources before travelling.

Arrival card and pre-registration: some destinations require an arrival card or an online pre-registration before landing, such as a digital immigration and customs form. Completing the arrival card or pre-registration before the flight usually makes immigration queues faster.

Immigration and customs: at immigration have your passport, visa or eVisa approval, onward ticket and accommodation address ready. At customs declare restricted items honestly; food, plant and animal products, medicines and large amounts of cash commonly have declaration rules.

Medicines when travelling: carry prescription medicines in original labelled packaging with a letter from your doctor, and check whether a medicine is restricted at the destination before departure. Some common medicines are controlled substances in other countries.

After landing: arrange local money, such as a travel card or a small amount of local cash, and mobile data through a local SIM, an eSIM or a roaming plan. Then take the airport link, an official taxi or a pre-booked transfer to your accommodation.
