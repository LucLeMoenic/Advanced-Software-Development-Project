# Vancouver — Where to Stay
Last reviewed: 2026-09. Curated demonstration content for the Accommodation Recommender: general, non-time-sensitive guidance only. It contains no live prices, availability, events or guarantees; check official sources before travelling.

Vancouver overview: Vancouver is a coastal city surrounded by mountains, forest and water. Vancouver combines a compact downtown with beaches, Stanley Park and easy day trips, and Vancouver is popular with visitors who love the outdoors.

Vancouver who it suits: Vancouver suits families who enjoy parks and beaches, couples looking for nature with city dining, budget travellers who stay slightly outside downtown, and nightlife fans who head to Granville Street or Gastown. Vancouver nightlife is relaxed rather than late.

Vancouver safety: Vancouver is generally safe for visitors in the main tourist areas. Vancouver has some visible street disorder around parts of the Downtown Eastside and nearby streets, so take normal care there at night and keep valuables out of parked cars. Safety for tourists in Vancouver depends on normal precautions: stay alert and keep valuables secure to stay safe.

Vancouver travelling with children: Vancouver is great with kids and children, with beaches, Stanley Park, the aquarium and Granville Island markets. Families in Vancouver are wise to pack layers for changeable weather and choose accommodation near the SkyTrain or a park.

Vancouver cost and budget: Vancouver accommodation is expensive, especially downtown in summer. Vancouver on a budget is easier with hostels, stays near a SkyTrain line outside the centre, cheap food courts and markets, and free parks, beaches and seawall walks.

Vancouver best time to visit: Vancouver summer is the most popular season, with dry, mild weather ideal for outdoor activities. Vancouver winter is wet and grey in the city but brings nearby skiing, while spring and autumn are quieter with changeable weather.

Vancouver neighbourhoods to stay in: Vancouver first-timers often choose Downtown or Coal Harbour for a central base, the West End for a quiet, walkable area near Stanley Park and the beaches, Yaletown for dining, and Kitsilano for a relaxed beach feel. Each Vancouver neighbourhood suits a different trip, so match the area where you stay to your plans.

Vancouver getting around: Vancouver downtown is walkable, and the SkyTrain, buses and small harbour ferries cover most visitor needs. Vancouver seawall is ideal for walking or cycling, while a car is only useful in Vancouver for mountain day trips. A walkable base near public transport makes getting around easier.
